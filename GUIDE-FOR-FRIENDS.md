# 🫀 Installing Traumader — in 3 minutes

*(Deutsche Version: [ANLEITUNG-FUER-FREUNDE.md](ANLEITUNG-FUER-FREUNDE.md))*

Traumader is a private dream journal for learning to lucid dream.
It runs entirely on your computer — free, no account, no cloud.

## 1. Download
→ https://github.com/phille2512/klartraum/releases/latest
Download the package for your system: `Traumader-mac.zip` / `Traumader-windows.zip`

## 2. Start it
- **Mac:** Unzip → RIGHT-CLICK "Traumader" → "Open" → "Open" again.
  (The warning appears because the app isn't registered with Apple — it's
  open source, you can read the code on GitHub.)
- **Windows:** Unzip → double-click "Traumader.exe" → if SmartScreen
  warns you: "More info" → "Run anyway".
- A black window will open (that's the engine — don't close it while
  you're using the app) along with your browser showing Traumader.

## 3. Set a password
On first launch you'll set a password. It protects your journal from
other people on your network. **Remember it** — the only way to reset it
is to delete the `auth.json` file in the Traumader folder.

## 4. Get it on your phone (optional)
Put your phone on the same Wi-Fi; the black window shows an address like
`http://192.168.1.23:8000` — open that in Chrome on your phone, log in,
menu → "Add to Home Screen". Done: an app icon on your phone.
(Only works while Traumader is running on your computer.)

## The daily rhythm

🧵 **The Dream Thread — how Traumader is meant to work:** feed it whenever
it suits you — harvest whenever you feel like it. No obligations, no
perfect entries.

1. **Capture** *(right after waking · 1 minute)* — a note, a phone memo,
   a voice message, anywhere. A few words are enough, only the **date**
   is required.
2. **Enter it** *(when you have time · 5 minutes)* — bring the text into
   the app unchanged. Tag features like dreamsigns, places, people,
   feelings only if it's easy — **everything can be added later.**
3. **Enrich** *(whenever · on the side)* — when you feel like it,
   complete descriptions, add elements you missed, place locations on
   the map.
4. **Harvest** *(when curiosity strikes)* — Analysis, Atlas, Inner World,
   a dream's Jung analysis. This is where the feeding pays off.

Feed it casually. Harvest whenever you want.

## 🔐 Where's my data — and what's the risk?

**Your dreams belong to you — literally.** Everything you enter here
lives exclusively in a file on THIS device (`~/Traumader/dreams.db`).
No cloud, no account, nobody reads along.
The flip side of this freedom: **if the device is lost or breaks, the
dreams are gone — unless you have a backup.** A backup is a copy of
this one file, or an export (Analysis → Data export). Do this
regularly — your future self will thank you.

**Rule of thumb: back up once a week.** Either in the app
(Analysis → "Export JSON") or by copying the `Traumader` folder in your
home directory. Two clicks, no more heartbreak.

## Quitting & uninstalling
To quit: close the black window. To uninstall: delete the app file —
your data stays in `~/Traumader/` (delete that too if you really want
everything gone).
